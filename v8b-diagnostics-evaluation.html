Metric Definitions

  • SMD Pre-TVT: Standardized mean difference in 28-day pre-event TVT between treated and control. Measures mean balance.
  • VR Pre-TVT: Variance ratio of treated vs control pre-event TVT. Measures distributional shape balance. Target [0.8, 1.25].
  • A/A Test In-Sample [-28d, event_ts): Cohen's d on the matching covariate window. Should be ~0 if CEM worked.
  • A/A Test Out-of-Sample [-56d, -28d): Cohen's d on held-out pre-period. Should be ~0 if no pre-trend bias.
  • Match Rate: Percentage of treated viewers successfully matched to a control. Target ≥ 80%.
  • ML Score Distance: Mean |logit_treated − logit_control| across matched pairs. Measures within-block affinity similarity.

CEM Blocking Variables (Segment-Conditional, 6-8 vars)

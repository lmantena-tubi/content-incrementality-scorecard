v9 Pre-Period IVT Bias Summary

Content Incrementality v9 · June 2026 (Organic Discovery, US) · 400 titles · 34.9M matched pairs · 7-day pre-event window

Finding: Treated viewers have systematically higher pre-period IVT (Intentional View Time) than their matched controls across all segments and 85–97% of titles. This indicates the v9 matching (which matches on TVT) is selecting treated viewers who are more intentional watchers — i.e., they watch more non-autoplay content.

Interpretation: TVT-matched pairs are NOT IVT-balanced. Treated viewers appear to be more "intentional" (less autoplay-driven) than controls, inflating IVT-based treatment effects.

SMD masks the problem: The pooled SMD passes everywhere (all |SMD| < 0.10), but this averages across segments with very different IVT base levels. The actual treated vs control IVT shows consistent +6% to +20% relative gaps at segment level. Variance Ratio (VR = Var_treated / Var_control) fails at 43–73% of title×segment cells — treated viewers have systematically more spread.

Key insight: SMD distributions for TVT and IVT are nearly identical — medians, P95, and max are all comparable. The only diagnostic that distinguishes them is Variance Ratio: TVT (which is matched) has 84.5% VR pass, while IVT (unmatched) has only 37.8%. This confirms SMD is not sensitive enough to detect the IVT imbalance; you need VR or direct gap inspection.

The Bias Pattern

SMD passes but VR fails. This means:
• The means of pre-IVT are close (small standardized difference) — matching on TVT does partially balance IVT means.
• But the variance of treated-group IVT is systematically higher (VR > 1.25 in 42–58% of titles).
• The direction is always the same: treated viewers have MORE spread in intentional viewing.

Why SMD is misleading here:
• Power Viewers average 3,300+ hrs IVT pre-period. A 200 hr gap is only 6% of the mean — the denominator (pooled std dev) is enormous, so SMD looks small.
• But 200 hrs of "free" IVT in the treated group biases IVT-based incrementality estimates significantly.
• Look at the actual IVT values and % gap in the Title × Segment tab to see the real picture.

Why this matters for incrementality:
• Treated viewers are more "intentional" watchers pre-event → they would naturally have higher IVT post-event regardless of treatment.
• This inflates IVT-based effects and partially explains why IVT DiD rates are higher than TVT rates for the same titles.
• The Power Viewer segment shows a +198 hr pre-gap (6.1% relative), meaning PV treated viewers have significantly more intentional viewing capacity.

How Pre-IVT Bias Affects v9 Results

Core Problem: v9 matches on TVT (total view time = IVT + autoplay). Two viewers with the same TVT can have very different IVT/autoplay splits. By selecting treated viewers from organic discovery (homepage clicks), we're preferentially selecting intentional viewers. Their matched controls (same TVT) may achieve that TVT via more autoplay, creating a systematic IVT imbalance.

Reading the gap: The TVT rate (v9 primary results) shows net displacement (rates well below 1.0). The IVT DiD rate shows less displacement because it removes autoplay effects. However, the IVT DiD rates are inflated by the pre-period IVT bias: treated viewers already had higher IVT, so even with DiD, the residual bias pushes rates up.

Power Viewer IVT DiD > 1.0: While a rate > 1.0 is technically possible (a title bringing in more off-title TVT than its own on-title TVT), it is rare and implausible at the segment average level. The +6.1% pre-IVT gap and VR = 1.257 strongly suggest this is inflated by bias. After matching on IVT in v10, Power Viewer IVT rate should fall below 1.0. The DiD removes level differences, but cannot fully correct the variance-level imbalance that remains.

Causal Pathway

1. Organic discovery selects intentional viewers: HomePage click → treated viewer has demonstrated intentional behavior.
2. TVT matching finds "same TVT" controls: But same TVT ≠ same IVT. Controls may have more autoplay in their viewing mix.
3. Pre-period shows the gap: Treated 7d pre-IVT > Control 7d pre-IVT across 92–97% of titles.
4. Post-period inherits the gap: Even with DiD, the variance imbalance means the treated group's IVT grows faster.
5. Result: IVT-based effects are overstated. TVT-based effects (which include autoplay) are more balanced but mix intentional + autoplay.

Recommendation: Match on IVT in v10

v10 Action: Add pre-period IVT (or IVT share) as a matching variable in the propensity model. This will ensure treated and control groups are balanced on intentional viewing behavior, not just total viewing.

Why Matching on TVT Alone Is Insufficient

Current (v9):
• Matches on: pre-7d TVT bucket, platform, tenure, content mode
• Result: TVT balanced, IVT imbalanced
• VR pass rate: 43% (IVT variance)
• Direction: treated higher 92–97% of titles
Proposed (v10):
• Add: pre-7d IVT (or IVT/TVT ratio) to matching
• Goal: Balance both TVT AND IVT distributions
• Expected: VR pass rate should rise to 80%+
• Cleaner causal estimate of intentional viewing effects

Implementation Options

OptionMatching VariableProsCons
A. IVT bucket Pre-7d IVT bucketed (like TVT bucket) Direct balance on IVT level; simple CEM block Reduces match pool (more constraints); may over-constrain small segments
B. IVT/TVT ratio Pre-7d (IVT / TVT) as continuous or bucketed Balances the intentional vs autoplay MIX; complementary to TVT matching Ratio can be noisy for low-TVT viewers
C. IVT + TVT dual block Block on both TVT bucket AND IVT bucket Strongest balance guarantee Most restrictive; may reduce match rates significantly

Expected Impact on v10 Results

With IVT-balanced matching:
• IVT DiD rates should decrease (removing the upward bias from intentional-viewer selection)
• TVT rates should remain similar (already balanced on TVT)
• The gap between TVT rate and IVT rate will narrow
• Power Viewer IVT rate should fall below 1.0 (removing the implausible positive incrementality)
• More confidence that any remaining IVT effect is truly causal, not matching artifact

Validation Plan for v10

After implementing IVT matching:
1. Re-run pre-period IVT bias check — target VR pass rate ≥ 80%
2. Confirm directionality is no longer systematic (treated higher in < 60% of titles)
3. Compare v9 vs v10 IVT DiD rates — v10 should show smaller (less biased) effects
4. Check that match rates don't drop below 70% (adding constraints reduces pool)

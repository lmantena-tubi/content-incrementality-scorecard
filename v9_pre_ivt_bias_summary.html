v9 Pre-Period Balance: IVT vs TVT, 7-day & 28-day windows

Content Incrementality v9 · June 2026 (Organic Discovery, US) · 400 titles · 34.9M matched pairs · anchoring matches the matching pipeline (treated → own event_ts; control → midnight of event_date)

Apples-to-apples read. This report measures pre-period engagement four ways — IVT 7d, IVT 28d, TVT 7d, TVT 28d — on the same matched pairs, through the identical video_session scan, differing only in metric (intentional vs total view time) and pre-window length. Both sides use the pipeline's true anchoring: treated windows end at the viewer's own event_ts; control windows end at midnight of event_date. TVT is what v9 matches on (the 28d bucket); IVT is the unmatched intentional component we are evaluating for v10.
Headline: With correct anchoring, TVT matching already balances IVT reasonably well. At the 7-day window IVT is nearly balanced (95% of title×segment cells pass SMD, pooled gap −3.3%, near-50/50 direction). At the 28-day window a small residual remains: control carries slightly more intentional pre-viewing (73% of cells control-higher, pooled gap −5.6%), but even there 91% of cells still pass SMD. This is much smaller than an earlier (buggy) estimate that had used a mismatched control anchor and reported 56% SMD pass / 91% control-higher for IVT(7d) — that reading is retracted.

The tell. On the matched variable (TVT), gaps are tiny and lean treated-higher at 28d (+0.6% pooled) — matching is exact. On IVT the pooled gap flips control-higher and grows with window length (−3.3% at 7d → −5.6% at 28d). The same viewers matched on total time carry marginally less intentional pre-history, and the deficit accumulates over the longer look-back. It stays inside standardized thresholds (all four segment SMDs |≤|0.07 at both windows) but is directionally one-sided — the classic residual of leaving a correlated covariate out of the match.

Recommendation for v10 Matching

v10 Action (modest, targeted): The corrected balance is good, so adding IVT to the match is a precision/robustness refinement rather than a fix for a large confound. The highest-value, lowest-cost move is to match on the 28d window (already done for TVT) and, optionally, add a coarse IVT-share / autoplay-mix block to close the one-sided 28d IVT residual and tighten Power Viewer variance. Do not over-constrain small segments (New, Reactivated) with a fine IVT bucket.

Where TVT-only matching leaves a residual

Current (v9):
• Matches on: pre-28d TVT bucket, platform, tenure, content mode
• TVT balanced to near-exact (100% SMD pass, mean |SMD| 0.005)
• IVT indirectly balanced: 95% (7d) / 91% (28d) SMD pass
• Residual: control-higher IVT at 28d (73% of cells, −5.6% pooled); VR loose on Power Viewer
Proposed (v10):
• Add: a coarse pre-period IVT-share (IVT / TVT) block, or a 2–3 bin IVT bucket
• Goal: neutralize the one-sided 28d IVT direction & tighten VR
• Expected: IVT 28d SMD pass → ~95%+, control-higher → ~50%, Power Viewer VR closer to [0.80, 1.25]
• Guardrail: keep match rate ≥ 70% on New / Reactivated

Validation plan for v10

Re-run this exact four-way check after v10 matching:
1. Target IVT 28d SMD pass ≥ 95% (from 91%) and control-higher share back near 50% (from 73%)
2. Confirm IVT 7d stays ≥ 95% and TVT stays ~100% (no regression on the matched variable)
3. Tighten Power Viewer VR toward [0.80, 1.25] on both IVT and TVT
4. Confirm match rate does not drop below 70% on New (M1) and Reactivated
5. Compare v9 vs v10 IVT DiD point estimates — expect small moves with narrower CIs (the pre-gap is small and DiD already nets levels)

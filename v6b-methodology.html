What does this measure? For each viewer who watched ≥5 minutes of a title, how much additional total viewing time did they generate on other content — compared to an equivalent viewer who never watched that title? The "incrementality rate" expresses this as: for every hour spent on-title, how many total hours of viewing resulted?

Pre-Period

  • Window: 28 days before event_ts
  • Purpose: Measure baseline engagement balance
  • Treated: Uses event_ts (timestamp-level)
  • Control: Uses TIMESTAMP(event_date) (date-level anchor)
  • Metrics: Total TVT, on-title TVT, off-title TVT, sessions, days active

Post-Period

  • Window: 28 days after event_ts
  • Purpose: Measure outcome engagement
  • Treated: Uses event_ts (timestamp-level)
  • Control: Uses TIMESTAMP(event_date) (date-level anchor)
  • Decomposed: On-title TVT vs off-title TVT

Step 2 — Matching Design

Approach: Coarsened Exact Matching (CEM) + ML Score Caliper

Two-stage matching: First, exact match on all CEM blocking variables (coarsened engagement buckets + demographics). Then within each exact-match cell, select the control with the closest ML ranking score (within a 0.2 log-odds caliper).

Why Three Matching Strategies?

Power Viewer + New (7 vars): These segments have high TVT or small N — exact platform matching fragments the pool at the tails. A coarsened platform_super_bucket (ROKU / MOBILE / CTV / MULTI) preserves match rate without sacrificing meaningful platform signal. Autoplay is excluded because Power Viewers' high autoplay variance makes it a poor discriminator.

Retained (8 vars): The largest segment with the deepest control pool. Can afford exact platform_device_bucket + autoplay_bucket_7d_4h without pool exhaustion. These variables carry genuine signal for habitual viewers.

Reactivated (6 vars): Dormant viewers — their last-used platform and autoplay patterns are noise, not signal. Dropping both yields higher match rates with no degradation in pre-period TVT balance.

Step 3 — Rate Estimation (4 Methods)

What We Measure

After matching, we decompose post-period TVT into on-title (viewing title T) and off-title (viewing everything else). The incremental effect is the off-title delta between treated and matched control:

Incrementality Rate = 1 + Σ(delta_off_title) / Σ(tvt_on_title_post)

A rate of 4.0× means: for every 1 hour a viewer spent watching the title, they generated 4 total hours of platform viewing (1 hr on-title + 3 hrs off-title uplift).

Four Estimation Methods

#MethodFormula (pair-level delta)Strengths
1 Simple treated_post_off_T − control_post_off_T Straightforward; unbiased if matching is balanced
2 Additive Proportional (treated_off − control_off) × weight_T Handles multi-title overlap (weights by on-title share)
3 Difference-in-Differences (DiD) (treated_post − treated_pre) − (control_post − control_pre) Controls for pre-existing trends; robust to level differences
4 DiD + Additive Proportional DiD_delta × weight_T Combines DiD trend-removal with multi-title weighting

Multi-Title Engagement Weight

weight_T = on_title_T_post / Σ(on_title_all_matched_titles_post)

If a viewer is matched to multiple titles, the weight ensures we don't double-count the off-title delta. When a viewer watches only one matched title, weight_T = 1.0.

Interpretation of Rates

Example: White Chicks has a simple rate of 4.11×. This means that for every 1 hour a viewer spent watching White Chicks, they generated 4.11 total hours of platform viewing (1 hr on-title + 3.11 hrs of incremental off-title viewing that would not have happened without this title).

The close agreement between Simple (4.11×) and DiD (4.10×) confirms that pre-period trends are balanced — the Simple method is not biased by pre-existing engagement differences.

Unmatched Treated Viewers — Characterization

17.5% of treated viewers remain unmatched. These are systematically higher-engagement viewers — primarily due to CEM cell exhaustion at the high-TVT tail, not caliper failure (ML scores are nearly identical):

Power Viewer160 hrs233 hrs12.3
Retained21 hrs66 hrs9.7
New (M1)14 hrs74 hrs10.7
Reactivated8 hrs33 hrs7.1
Implication for rate estimates: Unmatched viewers are heavier users with likely higher off-title delta potential. Excluding them makes the reported incrementality rate conservative — the true rate is likely equal to or higher than what we report. This is the safer direction (under-claiming rather than over-claiming).

Title-level match rates are relatively uniform (75–86%), so no single title is pathologically under-matched.

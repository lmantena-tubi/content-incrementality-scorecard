Content Incrementality — Movies (Sep 1–7 2026, Organic Discovery, US) v13 Presentation-Split Delta Results

Top 500 US movies by TVT · 4 viewer segments · min 30 matched pairs · 7d post-window (latest post ends Sep 14 2026) · Sep 1–7 2026 events · US only · 1.5% treated (device_hash_num < 15) / 3% control (< 30) · Variant C + a 9th exact CEM block on presentation · availability through Oct 15, 2026
Delta build: Incremental TVT = Σ(Δ/viewer × viewers × per-title weight) across segments · no additive-proportional rate · overall Rate = Incr TVT ÷ actual title TVT only

What v13 adds — presentation as a 9th exact block. Every prior run matched treated↔control on 8 exact CEM blocks plus the ML-score caliper, but nothing asked whether the control had ever been shown the title. A control who was ML-scored but never had the title rendered on screen had no opportunity to watch it, so pairing them with a treated viewer who did see it confounds opportunity with effect. v13 requires c.had_pres = t.had_pres, where had_pres is an existing presentation in [anchor − 7d, anchor + 12h) from core_prod.dsa.viewpres_vidsession_sample — each side anchored on its own anchor (treated on event_ts, control on midnight of event_date, the framework’s deliberate asymmetry). Because presentation is only observable inside viewpres’s 3% device sample, the control side carries device_hash_num < 30; treated is 1.5%. That asymmetry is the supply fix, not a bug. Every number here is therefore reported twice — once for viewers who were presented the title and once for those who were not.

The match-rate gate FAILED. Read everything below as provisional. The v13 plan set a gate of ≥80% match rate and said to report and stop if pres=1 missed it. Actual: 36.71% overall (96,792 pairs from 263,685 treated), 32.79% on pres=1. The caliper analysis put the ceiling at 79.62% even with the caliper fully relaxed, so this is control supply in the pres=1 stratum, not a tuning problem — three squeezes stack (3% controls, ~45% ML-log coverage on Sep 1–7 vs 66.8% in Aug, and pres=1 at only 7.56% of scored device-titles). This dashboard is published at the analyst’s explicit request despite that gate. The matched cohort is a non-random 37% subset of treated viewers — the ones who happened to have a supply-rich covariate profile — so the Δ/viewer estimates are conditional on that subset and the scaled Incr figures extrapolate them to the full population. Treat direction and relative ordering as informative; treat absolute magnitudes as unvalidated.

Viewer scaling is a pure 3% upsample. viewers = n_viewers_sample ÷ 0.03 from phase3_title_segment_viewers_v13. This is deliberately not step 9’s n_viewers column, which anchors on an exact full-US viewer count and splits it by the presentation share measured inside the 3% cohort. Rationale: presentation status is only observable inside the 3% viewpres cohort and Δ/viewer itself comes from the 1.5%/3% matched cohort, so the whole pres-split chain stays in one measurement frame and is upsampled exactly once, at the end. The cost is sample noise: the two methods agree to +0.283% on total Incr TVT (28,246,420 vs 28,166,592 hrs) but diverge per cell — median 3.6%, p90 16%, max 75%. The raw 3% counts are carried in the page (VSAMPLE) and shown on hover over every Viewers cell, so the evidence each scaled number rests on is visible. No cell has a sample below 30.

The Seg TVT and Title TVT denominators are NOT split by presentation. This is the single easiest number on the page to misread. sep_segment_tvt_hrs and sep_title_tvt_hrs are whole-population actuals — verified byte-identical across the two cuts for every title. So the Rate column reads “incremental hours attributable to this cut’s viewers ÷ the title’s total actual TVT”, not a within-cut share. The two rates are additive-ish rather than competing: for a title in both cuts, presented-Rate + not-presented-Rate is the comparable quantity, and each alone understates its own cut. Aggregates: presented 21.50M Incr TVT on 61.09M actual title TVT (35.2%), not presented 6.74M on 42.01M (16.1%).

The pooled all cut has been dropped entirely (analyst decision). There is no pooled rank tab and no pooled header figure — only pres1 and pres0. The consequence to hold in mind: of 333 reportable titles, only 110 appear in both cuts. 177 are presented-only and 46 are not-presented-only, so for two thirds of the catalogue this page shows a deliberately one-sided view rather than a title total. The Presented-vs-Not-Presented tab can only compare the 110.

Cells are thin, by arithmetic rather than by failure. 96,792 pairs spread over 500 titles × 4 segments × 2 cuts leaves a median of 70 pairs per reportable cell (p90 220, max 1,337). Min-pairs therefore defaults to 30, not the usual 100 — raising it to 100 discards 62% of presented cells and 75% of not-presented ones. Segment coverage is lopsided: presented has 287 Retained / 136 Power Viewer / 28 New (M1) / 11 Reactivated cells; not-presented has no Reactivated cell at all and only 3 New (M1). Any read of this page is effectively a read of Retained and Power Viewer.

Incremental IVT flips sign between the cuts. Presented is +2.09M hrs (Retained +3.61M, Power Viewer −1.76M); not-presented is −4.72M (Power Viewer −2.80M, Retained −1.92M) against +6.74M Incr TVT. Read literally: among viewers who were shown the title, an organic start adds deliberate watching for Retained viewers; among viewers who were not shown it, the added hours are system-driven and displace intentional viewing. Power Viewer IVT is negative in both cuts, consistent with every prior run. The IVT tabs are the honest denominator for “did this title create deliberate watching”.

Confidence badges are not coloured in this build. The v13 diagnostics notebook has not been published to dsa-lab/content-incrementality/scorecard/v13/, so there is no scorecard to grade (title × segment) cells against. Per-segment Δ/viewer badges still colour by sign, which is independent of the scorecard. No series leak to disclose: v13 screens the universe with a program-level join to dsa.dsac_program_info, so the 14 series-typed IDs that needed an export-layer patch in v10e/v12 are absent by construction — the builder asserts this rather than assuming it (500 titles, 0 non-movie).

TVT incrementality, viewers who WERE presented the title (pres_cut = 'pres1'). Built from the per-viewer simple delta — no additive-proportional rate. Δ/viewer = net total (on+off) TVT, treated − matched control, hours per viewer (winsorized, phase3_title_segment_simple_delta_v13_tvt); colour = sign (green positive, red negative). Incr TVT = Σ(Δ/viewer × segment viewers × per-title weight) across segments, where viewers = the pres1 3% sample count ÷ 0.03 and the weight is computed per (title × segment × cut) from that movie’s own matched-pair viewer distribution on the 7d pre-TVT de-duplication ladder (phase3_title_segment_applied_weight_v13). Pooled pres1 fallback: 0.363 Retained, 0.368 New (M1), 0.496 Reactivated, 0.148 Power Viewer. Rate = Incr TVT ÷ the title’s total actual TVT — the denominator is not restricted to presented viewing, so this is a share of the whole title, not of this cut. IVT incrementality, viewers who WERE presented the title. Built from the per-viewer simple IVT delta (phase3_title_segment_simple_delta_v13_ivt) — intentional view time, which excludes system-driven autoplay without demonstrated intent. The IVT build changes the numerator only: the weight, the viewer counts and the whole-title TVT denominator are identical to the TVT tab, so this Rate is directly comparable to it. Total presented Incr IVT is +2.09M hrs against +21.50M Incr TVT — i.e. roughly a tenth of the incremental hours from presented viewers are intentional, and Power Viewer is negative (−1.76M) while Retained carries the positive (+3.61M).

IVT incrementality, viewers who were NOT presented the title. This is the cut where the sign flips: total Incr IVT is −4.72M hrs against +6.74M Incr TVT, with Power Viewer −2.80M and Retained −1.92M — both negative, unlike the presented cut where Retained is +3.61M. The literal reading: when a viewer starts a movie they were never shown, the extra watch time it generates over the next 7 days is system-driven and comes at the expense of intentional viewing. Because this is the smaller, thinner cut (156 titles, no Reactivated cell, 3 New (M1)) and it sits behind a failed match-rate gate, treat the magnitude as directional only.

Does having been shown the title change its incremental effect? That is the question v13 exists to answer, and this tab is the answer.
The table is the whole cohort: per segment, the two cuts side by side (cells, pairs, median Δ/viewer, total Incr), and then the paired column — restricted to (title × segment) cells that clear the min-pairs bar in both cuts, so the presented-vs-not difference is measured within the same title rather than across two different title sets.
The scatter plots those paired cells: X = Δ/viewer among viewers not presented, Y = Δ/viewer among viewers presented. The dashed diagonal is presented = not presented (presentation made no difference to the per-viewer effect). Above it, being shown the title amplified the effect; below it, the un-shown viewers responded more strongly — which, if it dominates, points at self-selection: a viewer who finds a movie without being shown it wanted it more.
Coverage limit: only 110 of 333 titles appear in both cuts (177 presented-only, 46 not-presented-only), and the pairing requirement bites hardest on the sparse segments — there is no not-presented Reactivated cell anywhere, so that segment can never appear here.

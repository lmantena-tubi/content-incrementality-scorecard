Metric Definitions

  • SMD Pre-TVT / Pre-IVT: Standardized mean difference in 28-day pre-event TVT (resp. IVT) between treated and control. Measures mean balance. Both are mandatory gates.
  • VR Pre-TVT / Pre-IVT: Variance ratio of treated vs control pre-event TVT (resp. IVT). Measures distributional shape balance. Target [0.8, 1.25].
  • A/A In-Sample (TVT & IVT) [-28d, event_ts): Cohen's d on the matching covariate window — equals the pre-period SMD alias. Should be ~0 if CEM worked.
  • A/A Out-of-Sample (TVT & IVT) [-56d, -28d): Cohen's d on held-out pre-period, pooled-SD standardizer. Should be ~0 if no pre-trend bias.
  • CI-TVT / CI-IVT Half-Width: Half-width of 95% CI on per-viewer incremental TVT (resp. IVT) in hours, = 1.96 × step-7 winsorized SE. Measures estimate precision. Target ≤ 1 hr/viewer. Both are mandatory gates.
  • Match Rate: Percentage of treated viewers successfully matched to a control. Target ≥ 80%.
  • ML Score Distance: Mean |logit_treated − logit_control| across matched pairs. Informational.

CEM Blocking Variables (Variant C — no hard IVT block)

  • Exact blocks: title_id, event_date, user_segment, is_registered, tenure_bucket, search_visit_7d, sessions_bucket_v10d, platform_super_bucket, tvt_bucket
  • No IVT block — IVT balanced via a continuous ABS(Δw_ivt) rank tie-break (the Variant C definition).
  • TVT bin widths (per-segment): Power div=10/cap=120, Retained div=4/cap=40, New & Reactivated div=30/cap=90.
  • Segment matching window: 7d TVT+IVT for Power & Retained; 28d for New (M1) & Reactivated.
  • Stepwise ML caliper: prefer |Δlogit| ≤ 0.1, ceiling ≤ 0.2.

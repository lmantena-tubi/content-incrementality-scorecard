Where does the held-back viewing come from? Post-exposure TVT across all 153 titles, split by the session's originating page source (definition lifted verbatim from the v8b_page_source_pivot notebook — Deeplink / autoplay / YMAL / HomePage / Search / MyStuff / DetailsPage / etc., long tail rolled into Other). Share of Hold % = each source's slice of total hold-arm TVT. Ratio = Hold ÷ Control for that source (lower = the holdback suppressed that channel harder). The signature of a holdback: HomePage and autoplay ratios collapse (those surfacing channels were suppressed), while Search and Deeplink hold up near ~100%+ (users actively seek the title out — that demand is incremental / self-driven).

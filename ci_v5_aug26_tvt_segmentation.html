TL;DR — "Top TVT had the lowest TVT" is real in point estimate (−0.64% vs −0.50%) but sits inside the noise band: the two 95% CIs (±0.45%) overlap almost entirely, so the arms are statistically indistinguishable on total TVT. Two forces explain essentially the whole 0.14pp gap — a pre-experiment baseline draw (which CURE corrects) and one genuine content effect (non-substitutable kids-&-family animation held back in the Top TVT arm).
1 · Pre-experiment bias — baseline (pre-28d) TVT balance
Trailing-28-day TVT per device measured at each device's own first_exposure_ds from device_okr_daily (the same covariate CURE adjusts on). Randomization is clean — no imbalance is statistically significant — but the Top TVT arm drew a slightly lighter-viewing population, starting 0.134% below the Incremental arm. That residual baseline gap is the same size and direction as the 0.14pp post-period gap: nearly the entire observed "Top TVT is worse" difference is a pre-existing draw, not a treatment effect.
2 · Variance reduction (CURE) — why the ranking should converge / flip
The report ran with CURE: Yes. Pre-period TVT has a huge spread (SD ≈ 58 hr against a mean of 26 hr), so raw between-arm noise dwarfs a sub-1% effect. CURE regresses out that pre-period covariate, which (a) credits Top TVT for starting lighter, pulling the arms toward parity, and (b) tightens the confidence intervals. Because the raw gap (~0.13% of baseline) is itself within that noise, post-CURE the Incremental / Top TVT ordering is expected to be within-noise and may flip — the −0.64% is not a robust ranking signal.
3 · The one genuine content difference — non-substitutable kids & family
Where the arms differ for a real reason is the held-back title mix, visible in the whole-catalog ITT genre tests (mean over the full arm, non-viewers = 0; BH-FDR corrected). The Top TVT arm holds the big animated kids blockbusters — Trolls (123k grand hrs), Cloudy With a Chance of Meatballs 2 (79k), Monsters vs. Aliens (66k) — all bucketed kids & family. When those are held back, demand does not substitute to the rest of the catalog: kids & family stays −3.5% and significant. The Incremental arm instead holds action / fantasy franchises (Lethal Weapon, Colombiana, Twilight, Godzilla) whose viewers substitute within-genre, so kids & family even shows positive spillover (+3.7%). That single structural difference costs the Top TVT arm an extra ~48,300 net TVT hours (whole-catalog: Incremental TVT 96.7% vs Top TVT 96.3% of Control).
Bottom line
• The −0.64% vs −0.50% ranking is inside the noise — CIs overlap; not a reliable "Top TVT is worse" conclusion.
• ~0.13pp of the 0.14pp gap is pre-experiment baseline bias (Top TVT drew a lighter population); CURE corrects most of it and tightens the CIs, so the arms should converge.
• The only real content driver is non-substitutable kids & family animation concentrated in the Top TVT arm (−3.5% net, ~48k lost hours), vs. franchise titles in the Incremental arm that substitute within-genre.

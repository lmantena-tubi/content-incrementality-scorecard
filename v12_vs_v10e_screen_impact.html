v12 vs v10e — impact of the treated contamination screen

Movies · top-2000 US titles · Aug 8–14 2026 events · Organic Discovery · US only · Variant C matching (not re-run) · delta build: Incr TVT = Σ(Δ/viewer × viewers × per-title weight)
Baseline 10e_deltaresults_8Aug_14Aug.html (v10e_a14, 7d post, unscreened) vs v12_28d_deltaresults.html (v12, screened) · generated 2026-09-28

Verdict: the screen is close to a null result on movies. Removing matched treated viewers with prior on-title viewing (7d pre for Power / Retained, 28d for New / Reactivated) drops 6.2% of pairs and 7.1% of the extrapolation base, and total 7d Incr TVT falls 5.0% — almost entirely from the smaller viewer base. The per-viewer effect actually rises +2.5%. v10e was not materially biased by the missing screen. v12's real contribution is the 28-day post window, not a corrected headline.

Screened-out re-watchers had a lower delta than clean first-timers in Retained and New (removing them helps) and a slightly higher one in Power and Reactivated (removing them hurts). Both are within a few percent and offset. Per-(title × segment) de-dup weights are unchanged to 3 dp (median Retained 0.299, Power 0.128, New 0.300→0.301, Reactivated 0.452→0.455); 0 of 6,423 cells fall back to the pooled SEG_WEIGHT.

Contradicts the spec's stated rationale. MATCHING_SPEC.md predicted the treated/control asymmetry inflates the measured on-title effect. Empirically it does the opposite and by a negligible amount. The screen mattered for series (v11); for movies it does not — 7-day movie re-watching is rare (6% of pairs) and behaviourally close enough to first-watching to carry no distinct signal. Amend the spec rather than leaving the prediction standing.

Two 28d caveats carry over unchanged. (1) The de-dup ladder's constants were calibrated to spread a 7-day post, so over 28 days they over-weight — 90.21M is a ceiling on the direction, not a point estimate. (2) Rate is dimensionally mismatched at 28d: the denominator is the title's actual TVT over the Aug 8–14 event week only, while the numerator accumulates 28 days per event. Hence 293 of 1,955 titles exceed 100% (55 >150%, 6 >200%). On 7d no title exceeds 110%. Never compare Rates across windows. Offsetting these, the control no-contamination screen ends Aug 28, so controls can start the movie in post-days 8–28 — conservative, bounded at +4.0% by the _sens_ tables.

Where does the net loss actually land? The whole-catalog ratio is a single net number; this tab decomposes it into the three channels that produce it, arm-normalized so the hold arm and Control sit on the same device base (hold TVT scaled ×0.9996 = 3,405,839 ÷ 3,407,280 exposed devices). A. Direct held-title loss = lost TVT on the 27 held-back titles themselves. B. Substitution gains = extra TVT that non-held titles picked up. C. Cannibalization = TVT that non-held titles lost (demand that leaked away instead of redistributing). NET = A + B + C, and reconciles to the whole-catalog Net Δ. The headline metric is the refill rate = (B + C) ÷ |A|: how much of the hole the rest of the catalog refills. A higher refill rate = a smaller residual net loss for the same-sized hole.

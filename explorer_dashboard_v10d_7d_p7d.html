Mean trajectory of matched treatment vs control viewers. Day 0 = viewer's event date. Pre-period overlap demonstrates matching quality; post-period divergence shows incrementality. Band = ±1 SE.
Left: What control viewers watch instead (substitution) — top titles by avg viewing per viewer. Right: What treatment viewers watch after the event (amplification). Treatment title highlighted in orange.
Note: Both deltas use the same denominator — control off-title viewing per viewer. Delta (on + off title) = (treatment on-title + off-title − control off-title) / control off-title; it is the net incrementality including the treatment title's own (focal) viewing. Off-title Delta = (treatment off-title − control off-title) / control off-title; it is the substitution-only effect, excluding the treatment title.
Scope: the stat cards above use total (full-catalog) viewing per viewer for the selected segment; the bar charts below show only each arm's top 30 alt-titles, so bar sums are smaller than the card totals.

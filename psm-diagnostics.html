Key Findings

Strengths

  • Match coverage: 99.97% treated match rate — virtually no selection from coverage gaps
  • Precision: All 50 titles have CI width ≤ 4%; median 2.65%
  • A/A test: 92% of titles pass out-of-sample temporal A/A test (no pre-trend contamination)
  • Volume: 3.16M matched pairs across top 50 titles

Concerns

  • SMD violations: 50% of titles have |SMD| ≥ 0.10 — treated viewers are systematically heavier watchers
  • Variance ratio: 60% fail VR pre-TVT check — distributional shape mismatch between groups
  • Action titles dominate LOW: Genre-correlated confounding not fully absorbed by propensity
  • Robustness: 0/50 titles robust at Γ ≥ 2.0 — effects sensitive to moderate hidden bias

Interpretation

The matching pipeline achieves excellent coverage and precision but shows systematic imbalance: treated (completers) are heavier viewers than controls. This is expected given the completion-event definition — completing a movie selects for engagement propensity. The HIGH-confidence titles (mostly comedy/kids) have smaller imbalance because genre effects partially absorb the engagement gap. LOW-confidence titles (mostly action) show larger VR and SMD imbalance, suggesting the propensity model's features don't fully capture what makes someone an action-movie completer vs non-completer.

Diagnostic Correlation with Grade

HIGH-grade titles share: SMD < 0.10, VR in [0.8, 1.25], both A/A test windows pass. They tend to be comedy/kids genres with higher substitution rates (83%) and larger pair counts (77K avg).
LOW-grade titles systematically fail SMD and VR checks. They are predominantly action genre with lower sub rates (73%) and smaller pair counts (57K avg). Despite worse balance, they show higher Rosenbaum Γ (median 1.79 vs 1.42) — paradoxically, the larger raw effect size in these titles makes them harder to explain away by bias, even though the bias risk is actually higher.

Metric Definitions

  • SMD Pre-TVT: Standardized mean difference in 28-day pre-event TVT between treated and control groups. Measures mean balance.
  • VR Pre/Post-TVT: Variance ratio of treated vs control pre/post-event TVT. Measures distributional shape balance.
  • A/A Test In-Sample [-29,-1]: Cohen's d on the matching covariate window. Small ≠ 0 is acceptable (matching balanced levels).
  • A/A Test Out-of-Sample [-57,-29]: Cohen's d on a held-out pre-period. Should be ~0 if matching doesn't capture pre-trends.
  • Rosenbaum Γ: Sensitivity to hidden bias. How strong an unmeasured confounder must be to explain the effect. Γ=2 means confounder doubles treatment odds.
  • CI Width: 95% confidence interval width on substitution rate (delta method). Measures estimation precision.
  • Treated Match Rate: Fraction of eligible treated events that found a valid control match within caliper.
